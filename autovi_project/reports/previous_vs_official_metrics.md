### Metric Definitions & Methodological Discrepancies

1. **Image AUROC**: Historical evaluation computed a single binary AUROC across all anomaly classes combined. The official evaluator computes separate one-vs-rest AUROCs per defect subtype against the normal (`good`) test images and averages them (`mean`).
2. **Pixel AUROC**: Historical evaluation reported standard pixel-level AUROC. The official benchmark intentionally omits Pixel AUROC, arguing that pixel AUROC is biased towards large defect areas and unrepresentative of component-level industrial inspection.
3. **AUPRO vs AUC-sPRO**: Historical AUPRO computed standard continuous trapezoidal integration up to FPR 0.3. The official benchmark computes saturated Per-Region Overlap (**sPRO**) with ground-truth component saturation thresholds $\tau_k$ specified in `defects_config.json`, evaluated across multiple fixed operational FPR cutoffs (0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.30, 1.00).
